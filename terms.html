Terms of use

Torcida is a collection inventory and personal journal for adults aged 21 and older. Tobacco is addictive and harmful to health. The service does not sell tobacco, arrange purchases, or provide health advice.

Your records

You retain your collection records and photos. Submit content you have permission to use. AI identification can be wrong. Confirm the exact label, line, size and other details before saving. Collection values are personal estimates, not financial advice.

Free and Premium

Free accounts include 25 cigar records, one humidor, 10 MB of cloud photos and three introductory AI identifications. Premium includes up to 5,000 records, 100 humidors, 100 MB of cloud photos and 30 AI identifications each UTC calendar month. Quantities do not count as additional records. Failed AI requests may consume an allowance if processing began. Existing migrated records remain readable and editable when over a plan limit. Additions may be restricted. Downloads and deletion remain available.

Subscriptions

The purchase screen shows the store price, billing period and renewal terms. Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled through Apple or Google. Refund requests follow the store process. Restore purchases using the same Torcida account and store account. Deleting your Torcida account does not cancel store billing. No lifetime subscription is offered.

Availability and support

Keep backups of important records. Offline device saving is separate from cloud sync. Review the sync status before changing devices. Usage limits protect service costs and availability. Contact support about account access or incorrect AI results. These terms and the privacy policy must be reviewed by the publisher before public launch.
