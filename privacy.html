Privacy policy

Effective when account services launch. Updated November 4 2026.

Torcida provides a private collection inventory and journal for adults aged 21 and older. Contact: Loading contact…

Device and account data

Device mode saves collections, photos and settings in your browser. Accounts store your email, authentication information, collection records, photos, plan, usage counters and support reports with Supabase. Passwords are handled by the authentication service. Cloud data travels over HTTPS. Account sessions use secure HttpOnly cookies.

Tasting tools and private groups

Taste insights and humidor timelines are computed on your device. Saved comparisons are part of your private collection, cloud sync and JSON backups. Private groups store their name, member display names, session lineup, ratings and notes with Supabase. Invite codes allow others to join. Before a host reveals a session, members can read only their own submitted notes. After reveal, all current members can read the submitted display names, scores and notes. Group owners can delete groups. Leaving a group removes your membership and submitted notes. Deleting your account removes groups you own and your submitted group notes. Group data is separate from collection backups and can be downloaded from the group screen.

AI identification and pairings

After permission, your selected image or entered text goes through Torcida to Anthropic for identification or pairing suggestions. Pairing requests can include the selected item and flavor details from up to 80 opposite-category collection items. Manual entry does not need AI. We do not promise zero provider retention. Do not include personal information in submitted images or text. Selected images are resized and re-encoded before sending. If you report an AI result, Torcida stores that result and your description for support.

Subscriptions and processors

Apple or Google processes store payments. RevenueCat verifies subscriptions linked to your account ID. Torcida does not receive your full payment card information. Supabase, Anthropic, RevenueCat and the hosting provider process information needed for their services. No tobacco purchases are offered. Torcida does not sell your personal collection data.

Retention and deletion

Your account collection remains until you delete it. Account deletion removes its active database records and requests deletion of the linked RevenueCat customer record. Processor backups and legally required transaction records follow applicable provider retention. A deleted account cannot be used to restore cloud records. Exported files and copies on other devices remain under your control. Unreferenced cloud photos may be retained for seven days to protect interrupted saves.

Delete your account. Account deletion does not cancel store subscriptions. You may export a full backup or CSV before deleting. Use your store account to manage billing.

Your choices

You can use manual device logging, decline AI transfer, export your records, delete account data, and choose whether to share a tasting card. Notes and location stay off shared cards unless you select them. A photo may reveal private information even when notes are excluded.

Children and permissions

Torcida is not intended for children. Camera and photo access is used only when you choose an image. This release does not need contacts or precise location permission.
