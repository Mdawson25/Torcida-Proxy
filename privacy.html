Torcida Privacy Policy

Prepared November 1, 2026. Applies to the Torcida web app and proposed iPhone beta.

Your collection

Humidors, bottles, wish lists, smoke journals, ratings, photos, appearance choices, and timer drafts are stored locally in the browser or installed app. No Torcida account or automatic Torcida cloud sync is provided. Device or operating-system backups may separately include app data under your device settings. Browser and installed-app collections are separate. Clearing site data, uninstalling the app, or losing a device may remove local records.

Optional AI features

Before the first AI request, Torcida asks for permission to share data. If you allow, selected photos, entered text, and relevant cigar or bottle names and preferences used in recommendations pass through Torcida’s Vercel-hosted server to Anthropic. These providers process requests under their service terms. AI output may be inaccurate. Do not include faces, documents, or other private information in lookup photos.

Journal photo attachments stay local unless you explicitly choose one for AI lookup or export/share it. Tasting cards are rendered on your device. You choose their notes and location fields before sharing.

Service operation and retention

Network requests expose technical information such as your IP address to hosting and AI infrastructure. Torcida uses a hashed IP value in temporary rate-limit counters, alongside global request and budget counters. These counters expire, generally within two days. A hash is not a promise of anonymity. Request content is sent to Anthropic; provider logs and retention depend on the operator’s actual service configuration. Torcida does not promise zero retention.

No advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, cross-app tracking, or Torcida account identifier is included in this beta preparation. Future changes require updated disclosures.

Camera, photos and files

The iPhone beta requests camera or photo access only when you choose those actions. It does not need GPS, contacts, microphone, or notification access. Location/occasion notes are text you enter, not GPS readings. Backup and card export use the system share sheet. Temporary local export copies may remain in the app cache until cleaned up by the app or operating system.

Your choices

Use Settings → Privacy and AI sharing to turn AI sharing off. This stops future AI requests until you allow them again; it does not recall data already sent. You can keep using manual collection and journal features. Remove individual entries using their available controls. Back up before clearing local app/browser data or uninstalling, which removes access to that local collection. Exported backups and shared files must be deleted separately wherever you saved or sent them.

Adults and beta feedback

Torcida is intended for adults. It does not sell tobacco or alcohol. During TestFlight testing, use TestFlight’s Send Beta Feedback option for privacy questions or support. Apple handles TestFlight feedback and diagnostic data under its own terms.

Before public submission: the app owner must add a working privacy contact and verify provider retention settings and the App Store privacy answers. See the submission checklist. This preparatory policy must be finalized before it is used as the public submission URL.
